MEASUREMENT CONTEXT

Measurements and diagnostic uncertainty

01 / MANY SIGNALS

Different measurement locations

Pressure deep in the well, temperature near the tree, valve codes and flow readings describe different parts of the system. None tells the whole story.

02 / INCOMPLETE EVIDENCE

Missing and constant measurements

In our captured examples, choke opening is constant in one window and unavailable in another. Constant readings can be legitimate. A catalog entry is not an available measurement.

03 / AMBIGUOUS CAUSES

Similarity does not establish cause

Similar signals and historical analogues can make a fault plausible. They do not automatically distinguish it from another physical cause.

      Inspect the architecture

      What the live demo actually exposes

      Available to Muse

      Initial sensor summaries, source-scoped knowledge graph lookups, restricted training-only historical analogues and structured claim submission.

      Not exposed in this mode

      Exact well topology and unqueried viewer telemetry. Optional live v3 can request up to two additional summaries from a verified raw window; audit-only v2 cannot acquire measurements.

      Enforced by Python

      Five-sensor accounting, caching and audit trails in the investigation framework; inherited summaries, exact numeric/citation checks, bounded repairs and spend reservations in this auditor. Validation is not physical certification.

      Frozen classification evaluation

      Direct Muse classification did not outperform the adaptive baseline. GraphRAG did not demonstrate a classification accuracy advantage. Claim-auditing pilots were small, constructed and correlated; independent engineering validation remains pending.

      Scope and limitations

      Source scope

      Documented generic sensor associations are not exact well topology. Published failure examples are comparisons, not universal or discriminating laws.

      Replay versus live

      Replay displays unchanged captured v1 events. Live v2/v3 invokes Muse only after explicit action, with fresh protocol hashes, request/token limits and a global estimated spending cap. No hidden chain of thought is displayed.

      Decision support

      No root-cause certification, autonomous equipment control, safety-certified recommendation, fine-tuning or simulated success. Synthetic mode is deferred; synthetic fixtures are software tests, not generalization evidence.
