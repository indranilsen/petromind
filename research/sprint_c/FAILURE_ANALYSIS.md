# What PR #33's preserved failures establish

Reviewed actual private host audits, accepted assessments, usage events and frozen
scorer output for c-13df, c-6f03 and c-80b2 under B/E. This document describes the
previous study; it is not an interpretation invented after Sprint C's outcomes.
Full private traces remain outside Git; PR #33's public projection pins host-event
digests and returned edge/document IDs. No prior score or protocol was modified.

## Citation identity versus scientific evidence

Both prior methods matched 9/11 atomic labels on three paired tasks, but only
7/11 satisfied the frozen evidence/citation contract. In each method two matching
supported findings omitted the literal model receipt required by the reference.
The governing model was present in the initial packet, so these were not missing
knowledge-access failures. For one conditional relation, B cited edge:39 and E
cited edge:13 plus edge:39. For the calibrated-flow test, E additionally cited
edge:30 and edge:36. Those returned edges point back to model:M1 as provenance;
the scorer does not substitute provenance ancestry for literal citation membership.

This establishes identifier noncompliance and differing provenance granularity.
It does not establish that Muse lacked the necessary conditional physical concept:
its concise public justifications explicitly described the fixed-flow relation or
constant-flow counterprediction. Nor does receiving/citing an edge automatically
prove the finding scientifically warranted. Actual source entailment, appropriate
scope and calibration premises remain independent semantic questions.

A plausible interface cause is that the prompt treated all received citation IDs
as members of one flat set without making governing-premise citation explicit.
The traces support that interface mismatch; they cannot reveal Muse's internal
attention or prove a unique psychological mechanism. V1 therefore changes evidence
organization and attribution instructions, rather than enlarging the fact pool or
repairing citations automatically after submission.

## Missing proof versus affirmative contradiction

The same two prior propositions disagreed with the frozen reference under both
methods: the calibration-independent initial physical conclusion and transfer of
a published example to a current service-line diagnosis. Their submitted verdict
was CONTRADICTED, but their justifications said that initial calibration was
unverified or that the publication did not establish the current incident.

Thus the justifications recognized the limiting premise or source scope. They
supplied a failed proof obligation, rather than an observed opposite pressure
change or verified absence of hydrate in the incident. The trace supports a
verdict-selection/contract distinction, not a claim that the model ignored all
uncertainty. Wording such as “establishes” is itself metaevidentiary and can admit
interpretive disagreement about what is being contradicted. A frozen reference is
not an independent semantic certification of that wording. Preserve the labels
and count discrepancies transparently; qualified reviewers must judge entailment.

V2 gives a general three-way decision procedure: support needs admissible support,
contradiction needs affirmative conflict at the stated scope, and unresolved
support/contradiction remains NOT_ESTABLISHED. It also explicitly preserves valid
supported propositions and actual counterexamples. It contains no case IDs,
reference labels, private citation lists or new physical laws. V3 tests that
procedure alongside V1. No intervention guarantees blanket abstention success.

## What this ablation can measure

A within-study rerun of V0 is required. Historical PR #33 cells are not substituted
for that control. Exact literal-ID compliance, raw label agreement and frozen
mechanical scoring are measured separately; none is renamed “scientific truth.”
We separately track lost supported/contradicted findings, reference-disagreeing
abstentions, unsupported causal support and incomplete submissions. Independent
citation entailment, premise usefulness and physical interpretation remain null
without independent review. The tested model does not score itself.

The benchmark was independently authored and frozen before the prior Muse run,
but this sprint is informed by already inspected failures. Its seven correlated
variants are development reuse of one synthetic incident, not new independent
incidents or an untouched generalization evaluation. A positive mechanical result
would justify a narrower investigation of attribution/decision instructions, not
production rollout or a broad claim about industrial reliability.
