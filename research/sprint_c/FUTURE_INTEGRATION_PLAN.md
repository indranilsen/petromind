# NOT AUTHORIZED FOR IMPLEMENTATION

This is a proposal only. Positive research results do not authorize production
changes. Separate explicit owner approval is required before any implementation,
configuration, deployment, capture replacement or demo integration.

Potential transferable ideas are evidence inventories that distinguish raw
measurements, governing premises and documentation scope, and a three-way verdict
procedure requiring affirmative evidence before CONTRADICTED. The research prompt
itself is not a production patch. Synthetic M1 rules, opaque benchmark claim
identifiers, model-specific required-citation lists and hydraulic assumptions must
not be transferred into real-data auditing as verified industrial facts.

Before proposing a production patch, obtain independent blinded review of citation
entailment and verdict semantics. Then validate a fixed intervention against a
newly frozen real-data development protocol with source-backed scope, actual
calibration uncertainty, unavailable measurements and supported/contradicted
controls. Keep original 33-case classification scores separate from auditing;
never tune against their held-out labels or reinterpret synthetic scores as
classification improvement. Use a genuinely new independent benchmark for
claims of generalization beyond this reused synthetic incident.

Protect the successful v5 demo by keeping its current code, captured inputs,
outputs and default routes immutable during validation. A future approved patch
should be isolated behind an opt-in research mode and compared in shadow runs
before any live path changes. It must not automatically insert citations, convert
an unsupported verdict into success, or weaken v5's diagnostic safety policy.

Proposed regression gates: received-only citations, model/document provenance
separation, true contradiction versus missing support, retained supported and
contradicted controls, five-sensor accounting, bounded repairs, scorer exclusion,
secret redaction, validated settlement before releasing holds, and exact replay
of current successful captures. Require no increase in unsupported causal support,
no material reduction in supported/contradicted recall, complete valid submissions
and explicit independent semantic review. Specify the real-data acceptance
thresholds before examining that evaluation, rather than borrowing synthetic
thresholds after the fact.

Rollback conditions for a separately approved integration should include any
fabricated citation/reading, scope regression, blanket abstention, broken capture
replay, tool-budget breach, settlement-integrity failure or material latency/cost
regression. Preserve the previous release and captured demo so the default path
can be restored independently of research artifacts. Do not implement any part
of this plan in Sprint C.
